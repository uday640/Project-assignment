# Why I Chose LSB for Image Steganography

I selected the Least Significant Bit (LSB) method for image steganography because of its simplicity, efficiency, and ability to conceal data without causing noticeable changes to the image. LSB works by replacing the least significant bits of pixel values with bits of the secret message, making modifications imperceptible to the human eye.

Compared to more complex techniques like DCT (Discrete Cosine Transform) or DWT (Discrete Wavelet Transform), LSB offers faster implementation and lower computational cost. It is ideal for experiments and projects that prioritize understanding the fundamental concepts of steganography, rather than heavy cryptographic processing.

Overall, LSB provides a good balance of simplicity, capacity, and invisibility, making it a practical choice for image-based data hiding.
